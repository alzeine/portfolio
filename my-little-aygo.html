Personal Unity Project

My Little Aygo

A small game about a very important car. After my Toyota Aygo reached 300,000 km and turned 15 years old, I started wondering what I could do to preserve it after the day it eventually stops driving.

Explore the project ← Back to portfolio

The idea

More than just another game project.

Why I made it

My car has been with me for years. When I realized that it had reached 300,000 km and was already 15 years old, I started thinking about what would happen when it finally reaches the end of its road.


Instead of letting the memories disappear with the car, I decided to preserve it digitally. I modeled the car and turned it into a Unity game that I can keep and play long after the real car is gone.

What I built

I created the car as a 3D asset and built a complete playable Unity project around it. The goal was not simply to make a generic driving game, but to create a digital version of my Aygo and give it a place that can live on.
